- **BEIR: A Heterogenous Benchmark for Zero-shot Evaluation of Information Retrieval Models** — The paper that showed dense retrievers lose to BM25 out of domain. Read it before assuming embeddings win.
- **On the End-to-End Solution to Mandarin-English Code-switching Speech Recognition** — Built on SEAME — spontaneous Mandarin-English code-switching from Singaporean and Malaysian speakers. This is your own linguistic environment, and it is where every off-the-shelf ASR system quietly falls over.
- **Common Voice: A Massively-Multilingual Speech Corpus** — Where accented evaluation data actually comes from, and what it still does not cover.
- **A Gentle Introduction to Conformal Prediction and Distribution-Free Uncertainty Quantification** — The principled way to put a confidence threshold on a model output — the machinery under any honest human-in-the-loop routing rule.
- **DeepSeek-R1: Incentivizing Reasoning Capability in LLMs via Reinforcement Learning** — The open account of how reasoning was trained into a model rather than prompted out of one. Read against the 2022 chain-of-thought paper: same behaviour, moved from the prompt into the weights.
- **DocVQA: A Dataset for VQA on Document Images** — The benchmark that defines what document understanding is measured on.
- **OCR-free Document Understanding Transformer** — Skipping OCR entirely and reading the page as an image. The direct alternative to the column-gutter approach already in Career Side Quests.
- **Precise Zero-Shot Dense Retrieval without Relevance Labels** — HyDE. Generate a fake answer, embed that instead of the question.
- **Not what you've signed up for: Compromising Real-World LLM-Integrated Applications with Indirect Prompt Injection** — The attack that matters once a model can read untrusted content and call tools. Required before M5.
- **Length-Controlled AlpacaEval: A Simple Way to Debias Automatic Evaluators** — The clearest demonstration that judge scores can be gamed by output length alone.
- **Judging LLM-as-a-Judge with MT-Bench and Chatbot Arena** — Core reading for Course 2. Position bias, verbosity bias, self-enhancement bias — named and measured.
- **Moshi: a speech-text foundation model for real-time dialogue** — Full-duplex speech dialogue — the model listens and speaks at once. The clearest treatment of why turn-taking and barge-in are architectural rather than cosmetic.
- **Taming Throughput-Latency Tradeoff in LLM Inference with Sarathi-Serve** — Chunked prefill. The paper that names the throughput-versus-latency trade directly, which is the trade a field engineer is always being asked about.
- **Selective Classification for Deep Neural Networks** — Abstention as a first-class option: a model that says 'I don't know' and routes to a human.
- **tau-bench: A Benchmark for Tool-Agent-User Interaction in Real-World Domains** — Agents evaluated on multi-turn tasks with a real user and real policy constraints — and pass^k, which shows reliability collapsing over repeated attempts. The most job-relevant benchmark in this list.
- **Who Validates the Validators? Aligning LLM-Assisted Evaluation of LLM Outputs with Human Preferences** — The criteria you think you want are not the criteria you actually apply — they drift as you grade. Core reading for the calibration module.
- **Why Do Multi-Agent LLM Systems Fail?** — A taxonomy of multi-agent failure modes built from real traces. Read before building one, not after.
