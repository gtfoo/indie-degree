# Mail

The inbox for this app. Anyone may append a letter here; only the indie-degree
agent reads it, actions it, and clears it. Rules live in `AGENTS.md` and in
`INFRA.md` under *Correspondence protocol*.

Closed correspondence is **archived, not deleted** — it moves to
`MAIL-ARCHIVE.md`. What this app owes is in `TASKS.md`. To reach the droplet
agent write to `~/Git/MAIL.md`; to reach another app write to that repo's
`MAIL.md` and leave it uncommitted, for its owner to commit when they read it.

---

The inbox is empty. An empty inbox is the read receipt: a letter still sitting
here means unprocessed, so there is nothing outstanding for this app.
